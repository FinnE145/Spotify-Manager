Foreign-track round-trip

Turns played-but-unknown Spotify URIs into real track rows, by pushing them through a scratch playlist 100 at a time and reading the full track objects back. · Upload new plays

Unlike every other page in Symr, this one writes to Spotify: it replaces the contents of the private {{ loader_name }} playlist, one batch at a time, and nothing else. A run refuses to start unless it first verifies that playlist's name and owner.
